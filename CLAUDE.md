# Notes for AI coding sessions

This is Sacha's personal Kingdom of Loathing code: KoLmafia scripts (ASH and JavaScript) run in aftercore. See the [README](README.md) for installation and the local development setup.

**Read [docs/aftercore-goals.md](docs/aftercore-goals.md) first.** Every script serves that goal: spend free, cheap, daily and expiring resources to gain items and Meat, and use them to reach #1 (top ten accepted) in the Museum for every Display Case item. A second goal is completionism: every non-clan IoTM maximally unlocked and prepared, including all its zones and every copyable monster in the combat lover's locket and other copiers. Weigh any change against it.

Further documents in `docs/`:

- [collection-objective.md](docs/collection-objective.md): Museum ranking policy, protected copies and item values.
- [target-register.md](docs/target-register.md): unbuyable Display Case items and where they come from.
- [resource-ledger.md](docs/resource-ledger.md): Tquilla's net-positive resources with a best use for each, plus bottlenecks and surpluses.
- [iotm-route.md](docs/iotm-route.md): the daily IoTM route.
- [resource-planner.md](docs/resource-planner.md): the shared resource planner.
- [shared-settings.md](docs/shared-settings.md): preferences, combat script and moods shared between machines.
- [daily-script.md](docs/daily-script.md): `smmdaily`, the daily run: commands, settings, tasks, what is built and what is not, and its offline tests.
- [diet.md](docs/diet.md): `diet.ash`, the daily diet (a port of tehtmi's calculator that replaced `diet.js`): files, credit, changes and checks.
- [daily-script-plan.md](docs/daily-script-plan.md): the plan `smmdaily` is built from, with its council papers in `docs/daily-script-council/`.
